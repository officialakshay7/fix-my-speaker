Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data Fix My Speaker ("we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you visit fix-my-speaker.org.

1. Information We Collect

Advertising and usage data: This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and cookie identifiers in order to show and measure ads. If we enable analytics, it is used only for aggregate statistics about how the site is used.

Contact form submissions: If you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message to respond to your inquiry.

What we do not collect: We do not collect audio data from your device. The Web Audio API used by Fix My Speaker operates entirely in your browser — no audio is transmitted to our servers. We do not collect device identifiers, serial numbers, or personal information beyond what you voluntarily submit via the contact form.

2. Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the speaker cleaning tool works without them.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to contact form inquiries
  • To show advertising that keeps the tool free
  • To understand general usage patterns and improve the website
  • To monitor site performance and diagnose technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdSense (advertising) — see the Cookies section above and Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact form provider — messages sent through our contact form are processed by our form service solely to deliver them to us.

Fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading the site does not contact Google Fonts. Share buttons are plain links; they load nothing from social networks until you click them.

5. Data Retention

Contact form data is retained for up to 12 months or until the inquiry is fully resolved. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. Contact us at hello@fix-my-speaker.org to make a request.

7. Children's Privacy

Fix My Speaker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at hello@fix-my-speaker.org or via our contact form.
